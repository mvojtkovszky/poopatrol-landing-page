Privacy Policy

PooPatrol, by Snaps Studio AB · Effective 2026-06-01

PooPatrol helps you track your bowel movements and spot patterns in your gut health. Because that's personal health information, we built the app to be private by design: your logs live on your device, and most of the app works without an account, the internet, or sharing anything with anyone. This policy explains what we collect, when, and why — in plain language.

The short version

  • Your logs are stored on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to sign in and sync.
  • An account is optional — you only need one to back up and sync your data across devices.
  • This is health data, so we treat it carefully. We never sell it and never share your entries with advertisers.
  • You can export everything and delete your account and all its data at any time.

Your logs stay on your device

Everything you record is saved in a private database on your phone. By default it stays there — we can't see it, and it isn't sent anywhere unless you turn on sync. Each entry can include:

  • The date and time, and how long it took.
  • Characteristics like Bristol stool type, color, size, and passage.
  • The setting or context, observations and symptoms you tag, and free-text notes.
  • Optionally, a GPS location — only if you choose to add one to an entry (see below).

Account & cloud sync (optional)

If you want to back up your data or use it across more than one device, you can sign in — with Google on Android or Apple on iOS. Signing in is entirely optional and not required to use the app.

When you sync, the following is stored on our servers, tied to your account:

  • Your email address (used to identify your account).
  • Your entries — all the fields listed above, including any location you added.
  • Basic technical details to make sync work, plus your subscription status.

We don't collect your name, profile photo, or phone number. Your local copy always stays on your device too — syncing adds a backup, it doesn't move your data away from you.

Location (optional)

PooPatrol never tracks your location in the background. Location is used only if you explicitly choose to attach where an entry happened, and only at that moment. You can decline the permission and use the entire app without it.

Analytics & crash reports

To understand how the app is used and to fix problems, we use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These collect anonymous usage events (for example, that a screen was opened) and, when the app crashes, technical diagnostics such as your device model, operating system version, app configuration, and an IP-derived region. The content of your health logs is never included in analytics or crash reports, and none of this information is used to identify you personally.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads. We never share your entries or health data with advertisers. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings, and upgrading to Pro removes ads entirely.

Purchases

PooPatrol is free with an optional Pro subscription. All payments are handled by the App Store on iOS and the Google Play Store on Android — the app never sees or stores your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation of your purchase so we can unlock Pro features and record when your subscription is valid until.

Permissions we use

We only ask for what the app needs: internet and network access (for sync, ads, and analytics), notifications (for the optional logging reminders), and location (only when you choose to attach a place to an entry). The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library, and it does not connect to Apple Health or Google Fit.

Reminders & notifications

You can set optional reminders to log your entries. These are scheduled and shown entirely on your device — we don't send push notifications from our servers. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app or your device settings.

Security

Data sent during sync is transmitted over encrypted connections, and on-device data is stored in the app's private storage. That said, no method of transmission or storage is ever 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security — but we use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices & controls

  • Stay local: don't sign in, and nothing is ever uploaded.
  • Export your data: export your entries from the app at any time.
  • Skip location: decline the location permission and use the full app without it.
  • Delete your account: deleting your account permanently removes it and all associated entries from our servers.

Children's privacy

PooPatrol is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Third-party services

The app relies on these services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase (sign-in, sync support, analytics, crash reporting)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, on iOS)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll post the revised version here and update the effective date above.
